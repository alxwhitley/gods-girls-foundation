# Homepage surface brief

- Scope: donor-first Astro homepage in Persuade mode. About and Donate remain navigation targets; this build does not create those pages.
- Audience and job: a donor moved by the founder's survivor testimony should understand the specific post-mastectomy supply gap in Kenya and choose to donate.
- Proof and truth: the founder's confirmed 2018–2023 origin story, the proper-fit supply categories, and faith-led mission. No invented founder name, impact statistics, legal status, partners, or donation platform.
- Direction: Sanctuary of Testimony; approved composition `.impeccable/mocks/home-luminous-threshold.png`. Memorable moment: teal sanctuary shadow opening into gold woven light around an anonymous illustrative survivor figure.
- Composition: slim header; 68vh split hero; supply-specific proof strip crossing the fold; linen testimony section with dated origin path; fit-education passage; final donation field.
- Fidelity inventory: woven hero atmosphere and anonymous figure are generated raster; cross, supply drawings, separators, buttons, and timeline are semantic HTML/CSS/SVG; core copy and controls remain semantic.
- Component grammar: square or lightly softened corners only; fine gold keylines; no elevation; high-contrast display face paired with a calm humanist sans; headline is roughly 4–5× body size; gold is reserved for light, dates, and primary action.
- Responsive behavior: the hero stacks text before imagery; supply items become a vertical index; timeline becomes a single vertical path; all actions remain at least 44px and content remains readable at 320px.
- Unresolved placeholders: founder name, real photography, donation platform URL, and legal/tax language.
